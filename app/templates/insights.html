Performance Insights

Statistical insights into how hardware and software configurations (CPU, GPU, cooling, kernel, drivers, etc.) impact benchmark performance. Only displaying features with at least 3 distinct systems contributing data and at least 2 distinct cohort values.

Variance explained leaderboard

Ranks components by how much benchmark scores separate across their values (η²: between-cohort share of variance). Only includes features where some component value appears on at least two systems. NVMe pad/fan toggles are omitted for non-storage tests.

Cohort spread by component

Only lists components that meet the same replication rules as the leaderboard. Components are ranked by a composite alignment score (η² plus signal-to-noise between cohort means vs within-cohort scatter). Tiers (strong / moderate / weak) are heuristic: strong means scores tend to track that split; weak means the benchmark looks comparatively insensitive or noisy for that part. The bar chart shows each cohort’s mean raw score, ordered best → worst left to right. The chart title, Y-axis, and table blurb state whether lower or higher raw values are better for the benchmark you selected (from its Phoronix proportion). Tables list cohort stats and every system’s raw score. For CPU / GPU, sync specs with flask sync-hardware-ranks-api or flask import-hardware-ranks; then run flask calibrate-hardware-ranks so reference ranks fold in your actual primary benchmark results (e.g. V-cache vs non-X3D). Kendall τ uses the calibrated ref; tables also show the spec baseline.
